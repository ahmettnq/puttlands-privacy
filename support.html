Puttlands: Support

A cozy mini golf game by TNQ Nexus

Need help with Puttlands? Write to sopor.developer@gmail.com and we will reply as soon as we can. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version.

Restore a purchase

Open Islands, tap any locked island, then tap Restore purchase. Use the same Apple ID you bought with. Nothing is charged again.

What is free and what is unlocked

Summer (12 islands) and the Daily Island are free. One purchase unlocks Autumn, Winter, Spring, all Moonlit islands and Endless mode, forever.

My progress is gone

Progress is saved on your device only. Deleting the app deletes your stars and your home island. Your purchase is not lost: use Restore purchase after reinstalling.

The ball is stuck or something looks wrong

Pause the game and tap the restart button to put the ball back. If it keeps happening, email us the island name and what you saw.
