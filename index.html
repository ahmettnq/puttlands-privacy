Puttlands: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Puttlands is a game made by TNQ Nexus. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it.

We do not collect personal data

The game does not ask for an account, a name or an email address. It does not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and it does not send any data to TNQ Nexus or to anyone else.

Data stored on your device

Your progress (scores, stars, settings and unlocked items) is saved only on your own device so you can continue where you left off. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Purchases

The optional in-app purchase is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. TNQ Nexus never sees your payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies to these payments.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any information from children or anyone else.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
